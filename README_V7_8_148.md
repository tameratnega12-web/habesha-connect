# Habesha Agenagn V7.8.148

Marketplace-style agreement flow added to Business Directory jobs.

- Approved jobs show **Apply This Job** instead of direct Call/Email buttons.
- Business owners receive job applications and can Accept or Decline below How It Works.
- When a business owner accepts, the applicant receives an email.
- The applicant sees **Agree** below How It Works.
- After the applicant agrees, the business owner and admin are notified.
- Business contact information is shown after agreement.
- Includes desktop and phone layouts.
- No new Supabase SQL is required.
