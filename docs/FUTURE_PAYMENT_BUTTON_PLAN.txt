FUTURE PAYMENT BUTTON PLAN
Project Base: V7.8.332
Status: PLANNING ONLY — NOT ACTIVE DURING TESTING

============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================

This document records which existing buttons may become payment steps in the future.

During the testing period:
• Do not display Pay buttons.
• Keep the current Accept, Decline, Apply, Request, and Agree buttons unchanged.
• Do not collect money.
• Contact information continues to be released through the current Agree flow.

When monetization is activated:
• The accepting party will click Accept first, then receive a Pay button.
• The second party’s Agree button will become Pay & Agree.
• Contact information will be released only after both parties complete payment.

============================================================
STANDARD FUTURE TWO-PARTY PAYMENT FLOW
============================================================

1. First party posts an offer, listing, job, trip, property, trailer, service, or item.
2. Admin approves it.
3. Second party requests or applies.
4. Admin approves the request or application when required.
5. First party clicks Accept.
6. First party sees Pay Service Fee.
7. First party completes payment.
8. Second party sees Pay & Agree.
9. Second party completes payment and agreement.
10. The system confirms that both parties paid.
11. Contact information is released to both parties.
12. The normal completion workflow continues.
