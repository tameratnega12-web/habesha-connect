V7.8.344 CHANGE VERIFICATION
Base: V7.8.343 Trucking Shake Scroll Fix

Root cause found:
- The trailer agreement observer removed and recreated its own top panel after every DOM mutation.
- The all-category next-action coordinator and native trucking controllers competed for first position on Home.
- This produced repeated layout changes, page shaking, scrollbar/thumb movement, and difficulty returning to the top.

Changed only:
- index.html: made the trailer requester top agreement renderer idempotent.
- index.html: stopped the all-category next-action coordinator for Truck Driver and Truck Owner roles, which already use native trucking controllers.
- README.md and this verification report.

Preserved:
- Trucking and trailer workflows.
- Agree, accept, decline, contact release, completion, and cleanup handlers.
- Supabase tables, SQL, statuses, approvals, emails, forms, and all other categories.
