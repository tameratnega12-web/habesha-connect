V7.8.0 Trailer Rental Approval Flow

- Added admin approval step when a truck owner/driver requests an available trailer.
- Admin approval sends request to trailer owner and notifies requester.
- Trailer owner accept sends final approval email to admin and notifies requester.
- Admin final approval marks trailer rented and emails requester to complete the rent.
- Trailer owner/admin decline emails affected users and returns trailer to Available.
- No SQL changes required.
